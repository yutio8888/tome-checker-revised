# Heart of the Gloom terrain art review (2026-09-28)

Six selected ImageGen masters cover gloomy and dreamy floor, creep, and blocking fungal vegetation. Requests were made as foreground, single-image calls through `tools/run_imagegen.py`; full prompts, source pins, original-tool paths, call logs, and receipts are in `art/production/handoffs/heart-gloom-{1,2}/`. Source identity and gameplay contracts were checked before generation in `evidence/heart-gloom-20260928/identity.md`. `selected-masters.json` pins the six selected source hashes and gate outcomes.
The pre-generation check used source definitions and native image references; resolved in-game Grid checks were performed afterward in the six live fixture starts, not claimed as prior runtime evidence.

Seven ImageGen calls count against the task's hard ten-call cap: six produced selected images and one gloomy vegetation call was interrupted by SIGTERM before an image or `result.json` existed. Its `call-1/` directory and interruption note remain. The next call in the same handoff produced the accepted gloomy wall. Both vegetation masters retained native RGBA alpha and passed the unchanged alpha, corner, and grounding checks. The gloomy wall fell 3.08px short on the left and 5.08px on the right of the 25.08px A8.1 margin; dreamy fell 6.08px short on the right. Both show complete silhouettes with transparent space at 48/64/96px; two exact `(asset_id, SHA-256)` waivers are recorded in `art/production/waivers/a8-edge-margin.json`. No threshold or unrelated asset exemption changed.

`export.py` makes 144 opaque 128px runtime tiles (72 per skin) from these six masters: two parity floors, 16 N/E/S/W creep border masks per parity, 16 connected vegetation wall masks per parity, and three ladder exits per parity. The creep masks blend generated creep with the matching floor, keeping passable patches distinct without implying an obstacle. A muted plum wash separates the gloomy creep from its similar brown floor at 48px. The vegetation master fills a solid wall cell; matching neighbor masks carry the material to shared edges, while interior cells have softer detail to reduce repetition. The three native ladder shapes are retained as the cheapest recognizable structure, tinted for each skin. Native up and wilderness ladder pixels are identical, so the wilderness exit additionally has a diffuse daylight pool. All direction and destination semantics remain in native grids, outside the image.

Parity is applied to each completed tile at the established forest-suite multiplier of 0.895. Mean grayscale luminance of the floor pair is 68.62/60.92 (11.23% difference) for gloomy and 132.19/117.80 (10.89%) for dreamy. The same multiplier is applied after composition to creep, walls, and all ladders; `tests/production/test_gloom_exporter.py` enforces a 10% minimum for all 36 parity pairs in each skin, including both floor pairs. Measured across all pairs, gloomy spans 11.00–11.34% and dreamy 10.82–11.06%.

`exports/runtime/` and `data/gfx/refined/gloom/` have byte-identical selected tiles listed in `export-manifest.json`. `review/{48,64,96}/` contains every downscale plus a compact `contact-*.png` comparison; `review/*-wall-field-64.png` shows a connected 4×4 wall. At 48/64/96px the wall reads as an occupied obstacle and creep has a visible boundary. The wall art does not encode faction, rank, selection or hazard. Live shader review and supported-cell statistics are recorded separately in `evidence/heart-gloom-20260928/README.md`.

72 new 128px runtime PNGs: two each of floor, 16 creep masks, 16 cell-filling vegetation wall masks, and three ladders. All 36 parity pairs pass the unchanged 10% gate; minimum is **10.92%** (`ladder-world`). The 64px review sheet's third row shows the plain palette: [48px](review/contact-48.png), [64px](review/contact-64.png), [96px](review/contact-96.png).
