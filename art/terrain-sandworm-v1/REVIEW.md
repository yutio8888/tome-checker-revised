# Sandworm Lair terrain art review (2026-09-28)

Three foreground single-image calls through `tools/run_imagegen.py` produced the selected opaque floor, native-alpha blocking wall, and native-alpha sand ladder masters. The complete prompts, reference hashes, original tool paths, call ledgers and receipts are under `art/production/handoffs/sandworm-terrain-v1/`; the pre-generation source/appearance check is `evidence/sandworm-20260928/identity.md`. This used 3 of the hard maximum 6 calls. `selected-masters.json` pins the chosen source bytes and final gate findings. No master was replaced or cropped to fake transparency.

The wall call initially failed A8.1: its right inset was 17px on a 1254px canvas, versus 25.08px required. Visual inspection found a complete native-alpha silhouette and transparent edge at 48/64/96px. The precise `(sand-wall, SHA-256 1314fa7b3dca5fa9c82ced2c2c7bf3040b6bb2234d24df71bb0d93a96e76f720)` waiver is in `art/production/waivers/a8-edge-margin.json`; no threshold or other gate was changed. The original failed wrapper ledger remains; its original PNG was copied byte-for-byte and recorded through `art_tasks.py` after the waiver.

`export.py` builds 40 opaque 128px runtime tiles: a floor parity pair, 16 four-way connected wall masks per parity, and up/down/world ladder parity pairs. The wall master supplies fine surface grain and a separate vertical cliff crop. The exporter filters out broad cracks, uses grain from lit sandstone surfaces, and blends it into a darker warm umber top. The N/E/S/W neighbour bits then put that calm surface on all joined sides; the cliff appears only on an exposed south edge, with narrow edge treatment on exposed north/east/west sides. An all-neighbour wall cell (mask 15) has no cliff. [The 64px wall field](review/wall-field-64.png) shows a 6×4 wall mass with a single floor-facing south cliff. The ladder uses one generated construction with distinct light treatments for up, down and world; the actual direction and destination remain native Grid fields. The passable floor has no raised obstacle or false hazard. Temporary tunnel Objects are not drawn by this exporter.

Parity brightness is applied after each tile is fully composed using multiplier 0.88. All 20 light/dark pairs have at least **12.36%** relative mean grayscale luminance difference, measured by `tests/production/test_sandworm_exporter.py`; no parity pair misses the 10% requirement. The parity-0 interior wall averages **80.05** grayscale against **110.23** for floor, a **27.4%** darker wall; its RGB mean is **103/75/46**, distinctly warm, and its low-contrast grayscale texture has standard deviation **4.69**. An exposed south face averages **45.86** at the bottom against **80.08** in the same region of an interior wall. These are export values; shader-enabled open and remembered screenshots give the in-game view. `exports/runtime/` and `data/gfx/refined/sand/` are byte-identical per `export-manifest.json`. `review/contact-{48,64,96}.png` and `review/{48,64,96}/` contain downscaled inspection views. Live 1920×1080 shader screenshots and runtime results are in `evidence/sandworm-20260928/`.
