# Spellblaze burnt terrain art review (2026-09-28)

## 返工

The reviewer accepted mechanics but rejected the first lava and tree visuals in `spellblaze-L1-lava-64.png` and `spellblaze-L1-open-48.png`. Those original screenshots and the initial survey remain under `evidence/spellblaze-20260928/`. The original dark lava was too close to scorched floor; the old tree read as a round shadow. Both runtime images have been replaced.

Two foreground, single-asset `tools/run_imagegen.py` calls were made from the prepared [handoff](../production/handoffs/spellblaze-rework-v1/): **2/4 calls used**, one tree and one molten pool. Complete prompts, identity checks, pinned references, call ledgers and receipts are there. Selected masters are [`burnt-tree-v1.png`](masters/burnt-tree-v1.png), SHA256 `fd213f8457f0928c9e7e597199ef7c3184ad7a6ffc1f16b2fe6601ab722d2207`, and [`molten-pool-v1.png`](masters/molten-pool-v1.png), SHA256 `b39fe66cd6b1eb5af7e39527797e672d8805a44154c296b62c095a6847988a7e`. The pool passed its opaque-floor gate. The tree's native-alpha silhouette passed the alpha, corners and grounding checks, but its top inset measured 7px against A8.1's 25.08px requirement. Its first call therefore remains marked as gate-rejected in the call ledger; after inspecting the complete silhouette at [48px](review/tree-review-48.png), [64px](review/tree-review-64.png) and [96px](review/tree-review-96.png), an exact-SHA, A8.1-only [waiver](../production/waivers/a8-edge-margin.json) was recorded and the same original bytes were selected with `art_tasks.py record`. No threshold or other gate changed.

[`export.py`](export.py) composites the transparent bare blackened branches and trunk over the established burnt ground. At 48px, its forked crown, vertical trunk and roots remain discernible as an obstacle; the old circular shadow is gone. The lava export uses bright yellow/orange molten fissures under dark crust. Sixteen N/E/S/W neighbour masks omit the raised cooled-rock rim between adjacent lava cells and retain it only against non-lava ground. This yields one connected impassable pool, distinct in hue and value from the floor. There is no damage icon because native `LAVA` blocks movement without an `on_stand` damage callback. [48px](review/export-comparison-48.png), [64px](review/export-comparison-64.png) and [96px](review/export-comparison-96.png) export comparisons and [live screenshots](../../evidence/spellblaze-20260928/README.md) were inspected.

The five pinned earlier masters and two generated masters are hashed in [`export-manifest.json`](export-manifest.json); all **42** runtime 128px PNGs have matching source/export hashes. Every one of the **21** light/dark pairs differs in mean luminance by at least **12.55%**, above the unchanged 10% gate. Final shader-enabled natural-FOV frames show median floor/tree centre gaps of **27.09%** at L1 and **27.49%** at L2. The lower gap than the first export reflects the new branch silhouette over the ground, while still clearing the live 18% check. The final two-level survey includes lava pools in both levels, 48/64/96px open, lava, natural and remembered frames, mode round trips and native DIG repair. The altar and altered special grids remain native. This is fixture validation of loose source, not an installed TEAA or continuous-combat test.
