The reviewer accepted walls, floor and stairs, but rejected the first CRACKS master: in the actual 48/64px game view its dark region collapsed into a thin diagonal scratch that suggested a floor stain rather than an opening. Its complete prompt, original output, receipt and master remain under `art/production/handoffs/maze-terrain-v1/` and `masters/maze-chasm-v1.png` for provenance.

The separate CRACKS master exports two parities. No gate threshold or terrain rule changed.
