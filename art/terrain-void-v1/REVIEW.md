# Void / spacetime board family review

The original three selected masters remain unchanged. Their foreground single-image `tools/run_imagegen.py` receipts, full prompts, reference hashes, and generated-output provenance are in `art/production/handoffs/void-terrain-v1/`. This task used no further ImageGen calls: total **3/5** (the added graveyard allowance was unused), no threshold change and no asset waiver.

`export.py` derives 68 opaque 128px runtime PNGs. The rework blurs the blocked rift master into a calmer cell-filling mass, keeps a small amount of source grain, and removes narrow highlighted border lines. Broad dark faces appear only where the blocked cell has no same-family neighbour. `space` derives from the same calmer rift, and the existing Abashed floating-rock tree composites are rebuilt from the Spellblaze burnt-tree master. Exact hashes are in `export-manifest.json` and `art/terrain-floating-tree-t5/export-manifest.json`.

All **34** void parity pairs exceed the unchanged 10% gate; minimum **16.31%**. Source brightness separation is **81.83%** for floor versus fully connected rift and **90.32%** for floating rock versus outer space. All 16 Abashed burnt-tree parity pairs still exceed 10% (minimum **16.80%**). The [48px](review/contact-48.png), [64px](review/contact-64.png), and [96px](review/contact-96.png) contacts show the final exports; the final shader-on screenshots are in `evidence/void-20260928/screenshots/`.

Same-map weather-on and weather-off captures in all three L1 zones establish that the hard dark squares are native zone weather particles. The squares appear above vanilla and refined terrain and disappear in both modes with `config.settings.tome.weather_effects=false`. They are unrelated to the board exporter; the native weather code was not changed. The refreshed 64px natural-FOV floor/wall median gaps are **80.20%** (Morass), **91.29%** (Abashed), and **83.08%** (Temporal Rift). See `evidence/void-20260928/rework-comparisons.json` and `lit-pixels.json`.
